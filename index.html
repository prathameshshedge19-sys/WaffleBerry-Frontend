The idea

Your life is more than photographs and files.

LegaRya preserves the stories, relationships, and personality behind them - the context that makes a memory human.

Built by WaffleBerry, LegaRya gives experiences, values, family history, and ways of speaking a place to remain connected over time.

Meet Rya

The intelligence inside LegaRya.

Rya begins as an AI companion. As memories, stories, and experiences are shared, she learns the person being preserved.

Rya can become your digital legacy, or help preserve someone you love. She is designed to stay grounded in what people choose to share - not to invent a life that was never lived.

How it works

A legacy grows through conversation.

  1. 01

    Share

    Tell Rya memories, stories, relationships, and experiences in your own words.

  2. 02

    Remember

    LegaRya organizes what is shared into a growing, personal memory.

  3. 03

    Learn

    Over time, Rya learns how that person remembers, speaks, and relates to others.

  4. 04

    Preserve

    Keep that legacy available for the people and generations who matter.
