What is WaffleBerry?

A home for the stories that shape us.

WaffleBerry is an AI-powered digital legacy platform that helps people preserve memories, stories and personal experiences for future generations.

Our mission

Technology in service of human connection.

We preserve human stories so families can remain connected to the people and experiences that shaped their lives.

WaffleBerry uses AI responsibly to help people capture and revisit meaningful experiences—never to replace real relationships. Privacy, emotional wellbeing and psychological safety guide how these experiences are designed.
